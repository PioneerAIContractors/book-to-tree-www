What you get back

  • A family tree

    A GEDCOM file of parents, children and marriages, ready for the genealogy program you already use. Every person links back to the page they came from.

  • Two spreadsheets

    One row for each person on each page, with names, dates and places as printed. The second gives each person one row, pages combined.

  • The full text

    Every page typed out, so you can search the whole book.

How it works

  1. 1

    Get a scanned PDF of the book

    Many older family histories are already scanned and free to download from online libraries.

  2. 2

    Upload it

    It stays private to you. We don’t share or sell your data.

  3. 3

    Download your tree a few hours later

    With the full text and both spreadsheets.

A head start, not the final word

Old books have typos, and anything that reads them makes mistakes too. That’s why every person in your tree links back to the page they came from, so you can check before you rely on it.

Questions

        

        

          

            What kinds of books work?
            
Printed family histories and compiled genealogies that have been scanned to PDF, including
              the many older books that online libraries have already scanned.

          

          

            Will the results be perfect?
            
No. Treat them as a careful first draft. Every person links back to the page they came from,
              so you can check before you rely on anything.

          

          

            Who can see my book and results?
            
Only you. The PDFs you upload and everything made from them are private to you.
              We don’t share or sell your data.

          

          

            Which genealogy programs can open the GEDCOM file?
            
GEDCOM is the standard format for sharing family trees. Nearly every genealogy program and
              family tree website can import it.

          

          

            What will it cost?
            
Pricing will be announced before launch.
